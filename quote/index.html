Trade supply — manufacturing for other pavers

Other contractors buy your pavers and lay them themselves. No labour, no site risk — volume instead.
Why this line is worth having. On installed work you earn on pavers and labour, but you can only lay one job at a time. On trade supply you earn less per m² and carry no site risk — and the yard can run flat out. One contractor buying 500 m² a month is worth more than chasing five driveways.
